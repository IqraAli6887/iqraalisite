About

Research at the intersection of language, time, and people.

My research focuses on Natural Language Processing with experience in low-resource NLP, multilingual evaluation, large language models, and vision-language modelling. I develop datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation frameworks designed to make language technologies more robust and useful across languages, domains, and real-world settings.

I have worked across Pashto, Urdu, Japanese, Swahili, and English, and I am currently investigating how LLMs can model change over time in longitudinal data, particularly in computational mental health.

Research interests

What I work on

01

Longitudinal NLP

Modelling temporal dynamics, state changes, and evolving human behaviour across sequences and timelines.

02

Large Language Models

Instruction fine-tuning, in-context learning, parameter-efficient adaptation, prompting, and model evaluation.

03

Computational Mental Health

Human-centred language technologies for analysing mental-health dynamics and clinically relevant behaviour.

04

Multilingual & Low-Resource NLP

Dataset construction, multilingual evaluation, and robust modelling for languages with limited resources.

05

Vision-Language Models

Cross-lingual grounding and evaluation of multimodal systems across languages, domains, and task settings.

06

Dataset & Benchmark Design

Annotation frameworks, shared tasks, benchmark creation, and evaluation protocols for reproducible research.
